# The Tarski–Seidenberg theorem in Lean 4

A self-contained Lean 4 / Mathlib formalization of the Tarski–Seidenberg theorem over the real
numbers, in the set-theoretic form: **the projection of a semialgebraic set is semialgebraic.**

```
theorem TS.IsSemialgebraic.image_proj {ι : Type*} {s : Set (Option ι → ℝ)}
    (hs : IsSemialgebraic s) : IsSemialgebraic (projNone '' s)

theorem TS.IsSemialgebraic.image_projInl {α β : Type*} [Fintype β] {s : Set (α ⊕ β → ℝ)}
    (hs : IsSemialgebraic s) : IsSemialgebraic (projInl α β '' s)
```

Here `IsSemialgebraic s`, for `s ⊆ (ι → ℝ)`, says that `s` belongs to the Boolean algebra of
subsets generated by the positivity sets `{x | 0 < p x}` of polynomial functions
`p ∈ MvPolynomial ι ℝ`; `projNone` forgets the coordinate `none`, `projInl` forgets the
coordinates in `β`.  Equivalently (`Semialg.exists_cells`), a semialgebraic set is the set of
points whose sign vector on finitely many polynomials lies in a prescribed set of sign vectors,
so the theorem is quantifier elimination for the existential quantifier over the reals.

Nothing is assumed beyond Mathlib; the development has no `sorry`, no warnings, and every
statement in `Verify.lean` depends only on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

## The proof

The proof is the elementary one of Cohen and Hörmander (see Hörmander, *The Analysis of Linear
Partial Differential Operators II*, Appendix A.2, or Bochnak–Coste–Roy, *Real Algebraic
Geometry*, §1.4), in a *relative* form: the parameter space is any type `X` with evaluation
homomorphisms `ev x : A →+* ℝ` from a domain `A` (for the theorem itself, `A = ℝ[x₁, …, xₙ]`
and `ev x = eval x`), and the semialgebraic sets are generated by the positivity sets of the
functions `x ↦ ev x a` (`Semialg (Rev ev)`).

| File | Content |
|------|---------|
| `TS/Semialg.lean` | the Boolean algebra `Semialg R` generated by positivity sets of a subring `R` of functions; closure properties, sign sets, finite unions and intersections, preimages |
| `TS/PseudoDiv.lean` | pseudo-division `C (lc g) ^ n * f = q * g + r`, `deg r < deg g`, over a domain |
| `TS/RealPoly.lean` | real univariate polynomials: constant sign between roots, strict monotonicity from the derivative, Rolle, signs at `±∞` |
| `TS/Cells.lean` | the sign vector `sgn S P t` of a finite family at `t`; `Occ` (a vector occurs) and `Before` (left of); for a derivative-closed family the level sets of `sgn` are intervals (`sgn_eq_of_between`, by Rolle and a minimal-degree argument), so `Before` linearly orders the occurring vectors and `Before ε ε` holds exactly on interval cells |
| `TS/Neighbors.lean` | point cells have a vanishing member; interval cells have all members nonzero; an interval cell either extends to `∓∞` or has a unique adjacent point cell on each side (the nearest root), with the sign vector constant up to it |
| `TS/Step.lean` | **the Hörmander step**: for `f` of maximal degree in a derivative-closed family of nonzero polynomials, the sign of `f` at a point cell of `S' = S ∖ {f}` is read off the pseudo-remainder of `f` by a member vanishing there (`ptSign_eq`); on an interval cell `f` is strictly monotone and its signs are `fsigns sL sR` between the signs at the adjacent point cells or at `∓∞` (`signs_cell`); hence `Occ` and `Before` for `S` are explicit Boolean combinations of `Occ`, `Before` for `S'` and finite data (`occ_iff`, `before_iff`) |
| `TS/Closure.lean` | every finite family of polynomials over a domain is contained in a finite family closed under derivatives, erasing the leading term and pseudo-remainders (`closure`, constructed by degree levels) |
| `TS/Param.lean` | the parametric setting: the actual polynomials `Px ev F x = g.map (ev x)`, the signs of the leading coefficients `lcs`, the effective subfamily, truncation representatives `repAt` (semialgebraic level sets), and the verification of the step hypotheses on a piece `lcs = lam` (`stepHyp`) |
| `TS/Induction.lean` | the *profile* of a subfamily (which vectors occur and in which order, as Booleans — a finite type); `profile_eq_Ψ` expresses the step as a function `Ψ` of profiles; the induction on down-closed subfamilies (`semialg_profile`); the relative theorem `semialg_exists_sign`: for a finite `F₀ ⊆ A[X]` and a sign condition `ε`, `{x | ∃ t, ∀ g ∈ F₀, sign (g(x)(t)) = ε g}` is semialgebraic |
| `TS/Main.lean` | semialgebraic sets are unions of sign cells (`Semialg.exists_cells`); the evaluation identity `eval (t, x) p = eval t ((optionEquivLeft p).map (eval x))`; the Tarski–Seidenberg theorem `image_proj`; invariance under renaming coordinates (`reindex`) and elimination of finitely many coordinates (`image_projInl`) |
